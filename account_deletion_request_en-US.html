Request deletion of a BookSpine account and data

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app. In BookSpine, open Account > Delete account.

Request by email

  1. Email us from the address registered to your BookSpine account.
  2. Use "BookSpine account deletion request" as the subject and identify the account email to delete.
  3. Never send your password, sign-in code, or session token. If you write from another address, we may ask for reasonable identity verification.

Email hajenet@gmail.com to request deletion

Data deleted and timing

After verifying the request, we delete the account and associated server profile, public content, authentication data, and consent history, subject to the exceptions below. One Stop photos and job data are deleted from Storage and the server when the account is deleted. Where available, we also ask service providers that received the data to delete it.

We process requests promptly and normally complete deletion or provide a status update within 30 days. If identity checks or a legal retention requirement takes longer, we will explain why and give an expected timeframe. Information that must be retained for security, disputes, or legal compliance is isolated and kept only for the required scope and period.

Personal library data stored only on your device, including shelf locations, reading status, and private reviews, may remain after server account deletion. Export anything you need, then remove it in the app or through Android app-data controls.

See the BookSpine Privacy Policy for retention and deletion details.
